Why this was built
Photography has never had a global standard. Until now.

I have been making photographs for twenty-five years. For most of that time, I received two kinds of feedback — people who liked my work, and people who said nothing. Neither made me better.

Shutter League remembers your photographs, sees what you consistently do well, shows you what you consistently miss — and helps you understand what to work on next. A standard to grow towards. Internationally. Finally.
