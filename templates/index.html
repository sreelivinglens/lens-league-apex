Why this was built

I have been making photographs for twenty-five years.

For most of that time, I received two kinds of feedback: people who liked my work, and people who said nothing.

Neither made me better.

I built Shutter League because photography has never had a global standard to help photographers understand where they stand — or how to get better. The lessons I have learned, and continue to learn, are what I want every photographer to benefit from.

Shutter League remembers your photographs, sees what you consistently do well, shows you what you consistently miss — and helps you understand what to work on next.

A standard to grow towards. Internationally. Finally.

What Michelin is for restaurants. What ICC rankings are for cricket. What FIDE is for chess. Shutter League is that for photography.
