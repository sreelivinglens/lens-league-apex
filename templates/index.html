The Founder

Photography has never had
a global standard. Until now.

Shutter League was founded by Sreekumar Krishnan to create a more useful way for photographers to understand their own work — through consistent evaluation, patterns, mentoring and deliberate practice.

Calibrated through live sessions where serious photographers evaluated work without knowing whose it was. Five dimensions. One honest evaluation. The same standard whether you are in Bengaluru or Berlin.
