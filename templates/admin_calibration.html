How to use. Upload or rescore the 10 reference calibration images across all four paths — Sonnet Camera, Haiku Camera, Sonnet Mobile, Haiku Mobile. Compare each path to the human panel baseline (n=77, R20). Target: within ±0.4 of human on 8/10 images per path. Do not rescore without a structural reason — model variance of ±0.3 is normal.
Camera vs Mobile calibration — important difference: The 10 reference images were shot on camera. 7 of 10 are valid for mobile calibration (Cherry, Landscape, Fineart, Dance, Silhouette, Monks, Portrait). Birds and Cubs cannot be used for mobile calibration — both require telephoto reach (400–600mm) that no phone can achieve. Sport is conditional on whether the image shows proximity action.
When scoring mobile track: weights shift (+15% Disruption, +10% DM). DoD uses proximity-as-discipline (no telephoto) and portrait-mode execution (no optical bokeh). Photographic principles — Ma, Kanso, Gestalt Figure/Ground — apply equally to both tracks. Mobile human baseline not yet established — upload mobile-shot images and run a separate panel before treating mobile scores as calibrated.
