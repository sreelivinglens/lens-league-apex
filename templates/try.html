Questions

General-purpose AI tools give you generic text feedback — "good composition", "nice use of light". That feedback is not benchmarked against anything, changes if you ask again, and does not account for your interest area. A Wildlife image is judged the same as a Street image.

Shutter League uses five dimensions that are specifically weighted for your interest area. The timing and moment weighting in Wildlife is very different from Street. The evaluation is consistent — the same image produces the same result because it is calibrated against a pool of already-evaluated photographs. Your percentile is real: it tells you exactly where this photograph sits against every evaluated image on this platform.

Other AI tools have no calibration pool, no interest area weighting, no body of work memory, and no percentile. They give you words. We give you data.

DDI evaluates five dimensions of a photograph, each weighted differently depending on your interest area:

Depth of Difficulty (DoD) — How hard was it to make this photograph? Right time, right place, right conditions. A Milky Way shot above cloud requires very different difficulty than a street portrait.

Visual Disruption (VD) — Does this image stop the viewer? Compositional decisions that break expectation.

Timing & Moment (DM) — Was the trigger pulled at the right moment? Behaviour, expression, peak action.

Emotional Impact (WF) — Does this image make you feel something? Emotional resonance.

Affective Quotient (AQ) — The intangible. Is there soul in this frame?

A full evaluation includes everything in the free evaluation — all five DDI dimensions, your percentile, your tier — plus:

Sherpa Narrative — A detailed written analysis of what worked in this frame and what held it back, written specifically for this image.

Location Advisory — Guidance on where to be, and when, to improve on this type of photograph.

Master Reference — A master photographer whose body of work is relevant to this image, with specific techniques to study.

Body of Work Sequence — How this image sits within your full body of work, and what the engine recommends you focus on next.

The full Shutter League programme is available as an annual subscription. Visit the pricing page for current rates and plan details.

Your 3 free evaluations are included with registration. They are processed using the same five DDI dimensions as the full engine, with the Sherpa narrative and advisory sections unlocked only for subscribers.
