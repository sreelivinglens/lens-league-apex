Questions

General-purpose AI tools give you generic text feedback — "good composition", "nice use of light". That feedback is not benchmarked against anything, changes if you ask again, and does not account for your interest area. A Wildlife image is judged the same as a Street image.

Shutter League evaluates five dimensions that are specifically weighted for your interest area. Your percentile is real — it tells you exactly where this photograph sits against every evaluated image on this platform. The engine is consistent. The same image produces the same result.

Depth of Difficulty — How hard was it to make this photograph? Right time, right place, right conditions.

Visual Disruption — Does this image stop the viewer? Compositional decisions that break expectation.

Timing & Moment — Was the trigger pulled at the right moment? Behaviour, expression, peak action.

Emotional Impact — Does this image make you feel something? Emotional resonance, awe, curiosity.

Affective Quotient — Is there soul in this frame? The intangible quality that makes it memorable.

Everything in the free evaluation — five dimensions, percentile, tier — plus:

Mentor Advice — A detailed written analysis of what worked in this frame and what held it back, written specifically for this image.

Location Advisory — Where to be, and when, to improve on this type of photograph.

Master Reference — A master photographer whose body of work is relevant to this image, with specific techniques to study.

Body of Work Sequence — How this image sits within your portfolio and what the engine recommends you focus on next.
